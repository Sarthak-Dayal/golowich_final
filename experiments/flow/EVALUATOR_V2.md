# Explicit noise rejection — execution protocol

User requested training the evaluator to reject pure noise. This run changes only the evaluator and re-scores the existing flow checkpoint; it does not train a new flow model or alter the pilot-v1 results.

- Preserve the original ten-way classifier, normalized feature embedding, class-reference thresholds, and confidence requirement exactly. Train a separate binary CNN, with the same two-convolution/128-unit architecture and a one-logit real/non-digit head, using fresh initialization (seed 851421).
- Positive training examples: the original 50k MNIST training split. Fresh synthetic negatives: equal proportions of raw Gaussian, clipped Gaussian, uniform, constant, pixel-shuffled training digits, short-range smoothed noise, and sparse binary noise. Real and negative classes have equal loss weight. Adam, lr 0.001, batch 256 real plus 256 negative; up to 20 epochs and 15 GPU-minutes total process time on one idle card. No flow-generated sample is a training negative.
- Calibrate on the original 10k real validation digits and 21k fresh negatives (3k per trained family, disjoint RNG stream). Set threshold to at least 0.5 and strictly above the empirical 99.9th percentile of negative scores. This score threshold is a decision rule, not a calibrated probability of being a real image in deployment.
- After at least three epochs, choose the earliest epoch retaining real-validation combined acceptance within 2 percentage points of the frozen original proxy, with no digit class losing more than 5 points, while combined negative acceptance is ≤0.1%. Stop by 20 epochs/15 GPU-minutes and label a failed calibration explicitly. Do not change settings based on test results.
- Freeze the rejector and threshold before testing on the official 10k real test digits and 10k new negatives per trained noise family. Separately test unseen phase-randomized digit spectra, long-range correlated noise, and shifted/scaled Gaussian noise (10k each). Negative source images for pixel/spectrum transformations come from the matching held-out split. Report per-family false acceptance, real-digit retention and per-digit rates, with sampling intervals; this is one evaluator training seed.
- Reproduce the old 512-image Gaussian sanity check as a clearly labeled historical comparison. Re-evaluate the existing 2,000 full-noise flow samples from the unchanged 10,000-update checkpoint, same 100-step sampler and initial noise seed. Preserve numerical-failure rejection. Do not treat these scores as evidence that generation has improved.
- Save a separate `results/flow/evaluator_v2/` report, checkpoints, raw per-image scores, source/config/data hashes, plots, and matched original/new acceptance grids. Keep pilot-v1 artifacts unchanged. Generalization to arbitrary malformed or adversarial images remains untested beyond the listed suites; retain visual inspection.
